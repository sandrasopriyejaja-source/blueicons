OUR COMPANY

Dependable Support for Everyday and Special Requirements.

BLUE_ICONS GLOBAL LIMITED provides professional security services, event ushering, executive car rentals, chauffeur services and logistics support. Based in Port Harcourt, we work with clients across Nigeria to deliver reliable, well-coordinated support.

Our focus is on understanding each client’s brief and providing the right personnel and transport. We believe in doing the job properly, with clear communication and dependable service from start to finish.
