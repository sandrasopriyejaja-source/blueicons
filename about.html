Our Company

Dependable Support for Everyday and Special Requirements.

BLUE_ICONS GLOBAL LIMITED provides professional security services, event ushering, executive car rentals, chauffeur services and logistics support. Based in Port Harcourt, we work with individuals, businesses and corporate organisations across Nigeria.

Every request starts with a conversation. We clarify what is needed, where the service will take place and when support is required. From security arrangements to event staffing and vehicle hire, we aim to make the process clear, organised and straightforward.
