BEFORE YOU BOOK

Rental terms & confirmation

Every booking is subject to confirmation by BLUE_ICONS.

Before you proceed, our team will explain the applicable rental conditions, payment arrangements, any required documentation and cancellation terms for your specific booking. No rental is confirmed until the details have been agreed.

Booking details and payment

Please check the rental dates, equipment, delivery or collection arrangements and quoted charges before confirming. Any applicable deposit, payment schedule or additional charges will be explained for your booking. Let our team know promptly if any information is incorrect or your requirements change.

Equipment use and return

Use the rented equipment only for its agreed purpose and follow the operating and safety instructions provided. Report any faults, loss or damage as soon as possible. The agreed rental conditions will set out collection and return requirements, including timing, equipment condition and any applicable charges.

Changes and cancellations

Contact BLUE_ICONS to request a change, extension or cancellation. Availability, revised pricing and any cancellation charges or refund arrangements will depend on the terms agreed for your booking. Please review these details and ask our team about anything that is unclear before proceeding.
